About Nail X Taytay

🏠 Home-Based Service Only

Nail X Taytay is a home-based nail studio offering gel manicures, nail extensions, BIAB, custom nail art, and gel pedicures — all done with quality products in a clean, relaxed setting.

Every appointment is booked one-on-one, so you always get undivided attention and a service tailored to what you want.

Home Service

Beautiful Nails, In the Comfort of Your Home

Prefer to have your nails done at home? Our nail artist can come to you for a convenient and relaxing nail experience.

2–3 Clients or More

Home service is available for a minimum of 2–3 clients or more in the same location, making the visit convenient and worthwhile for everyone.

Transportation Fee

The transportation fee is shouldered by the client and will be discussed based on the service location before booking.
