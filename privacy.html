Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Nail X Taytay ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use this website to book an appointment, check your referral rewards, or manage your booking, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Booking details — your name, mobile number, chosen service, date and time, and any notes or inspiration photos you choose to share.
  • Referral information — if you were referred by a friend, we record their mobile number to track referral rewards.
  • Push notification subscription — only if you tap "Enable Appointment Reminder", your browser creates a subscription (no personal content, just a way to deliver a reminder notification to that device) which we store against your booking.

How we use it

  • To confirm and manage your appointment.
  • To send you an appointment reminder notification, only if you opted in.
  • To track and honor referral rewards.
  • To improve our services based on booking patterns (e.g. popular time slots).

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not use your information for advertising.
  • We do not share your booking details publicly — the public calendar only shows which time slots are taken, never your name or contact details.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), a industry-standard cloud database. Access to the full list of bookings is restricted to the site administrator.

Your choices

  • You can cancel or request a reschedule for your own booking anytime using the "Manage Booking" page.
  • You can turn off appointment reminder notifications anytime in your browser's site settings.
  • You can ask us to delete your booking information by messaging us directly.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It may use your browser's local storage only to remember your own preferences (like whether you've dismissed a prompt).

Your privacy matters to us.
Questions about your data?

Message us directly through Messenger and we'll be happy to help.
